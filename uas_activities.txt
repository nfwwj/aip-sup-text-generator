This Permit is granted subject to the following conditions:3.
(a) The Operator shall take all necessary measures and precautions to ensure that the flight of the
unmanned aircraft will not pose a hazard to and / or cause injury or death to persons and / or damage
to properties.

(b) Without prejudice to the generality of sub-paragraph (a), such measures shall include the following:
i. The Operator shall ensure that the unmanned aircraft operation is entirely confined within the
area shown in Annex A and shall not exceed the maximum operating height specified in this permit
at all times;
ii. The Operator shall ensure that the unmanned aircraft maintains an adequate safety
distance and not overfly any person, public road, vehicle or structure unless permitted
under this Permit. This includes the launch and recovery of the unmanned aircraft and in the
eventuality of a lost link situation between the unmanned aircraft pilot and the unmanned aircraft;
iii. The Operator shall ensure that the unmanned aircraft does not interfere with the emergency
services or public transport providers and shall not endanger or cause distraction to the members
of public;
iv. The Operator shall use enhanced lighting on the unmanned aircraft for greater visibility for low-
light or night operation;
v. The Operator shall ensure that the Remote Pilot or Remote Observer(s) maintains visual contact
with the unmanned aircraft at all times;
vi. The Remote Observer(s) shall relay critical flight information to and assist the Remote Pilot in
maintaining safe separation from any static or dynamic obstacles;
vii. The Operator shall ensure that the Remote Pilot and the Remote Observer(s) are adequately
trained to perform EVLOS operations;
viii. The Operator shall ensure that the unmanned aircraft maintains sufficient separation with other
unmanned aircraft operating within the same area of operation or flight area if any;
ix. The Operator shall ensure that during the conduct of automatic / semi-autonomous operations,
the pilot shall maintain or be able to take over control of the unmanned aircraft if required to ensure
safety;
x. There shall be no more than one unmanned aircraft airborne at any one time;
xi. The unmanned aircraft shall not be flown for longer than 85% of the maximum endurance of the
model of unmanned aircraft deployed and an appropriate crew rest cycle for the UA pilot shall be
adhered to at all times; and
xii. The Operator shall cease the unmanned aircraft flight, if necessary, to ensure safety.
The Operator must submit the information required under the tab “Flight Request” in the Centralised Flight
Management System (CFMS) FlyItSafe mobile application immediately before commencing the unmanned
aircraft flight. After the unmanned aircraft flight has ceased, the Operator shall “End” the flight in the CFMS
FlyItSafe mobile application.
In the event of inaccessibility or malfunction of CFMS or FlyItSafe mobile application, the operator shall
inform / seek positive clearance from the RSAF 203 Squadron Duty Controller (Tel. No.: 6510 5333 / 6510
5335) on the operating height and area of operation, at least 15 minutes prior and inform again when the
unmanned aircraft flights have ceased. The Operator shall also provide the flight controller number and
serial number of the unmanned aircraft system for RSAF's verification purposes. The CAAS/RSAF could
also make calls to verify if the operator is operating at the approved location, without prior notification.

The Operator is reminded that photography or videography of Protected Areas or Protected Places without
the permission of the authority of that Protected Area or Protected Place is an offence under the
Infrastructure Act 2017 (IPA). The Operator is expected to seek permission from the relevant authority
before the commencement of flight if there is a need to capture photography / videography of
Protected Areas or Protected Places. The list of Protected Areas or Protected Places may be found at
SPF’s IPA webpage (https://www.police.gov.sg/Advisories/Infrastructure-Protection/Protected-Areas-and-
Protected-Places). The Operator is also reminded to check if they are operating the unmanned aircraft
within proximity of Protected Areas under the Air Navigation Act (ANA) and to be mindful of the boundaries
stated in One Map. The Operator shall alert the CAAS UAS Duty Officer (Tel: 6241 1292) immediately
upon any intrusion of the unmanned aircraft into the boundaries.
10.
This Permit does not permit the operation of any unmanned aircraft within a Temporary Restricted Area
(TRA) that may be established during the validity period of this Permit. A separate Class 1 Activity Permit
must be obtained in order to carry out an activity within the TRA.
